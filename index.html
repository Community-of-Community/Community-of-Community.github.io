CoC helps community founders and builders notice one another, exchange a useful spark, and try a small collaboration when it feels right.

What CoC is (and isn’t)＋

We create space for introductions, mutual learning, and small public-benefit collaborations between independent communities.

We do not run member communities, broker commercial access, or speak on anyone’s behalf. Participation is voluntary; privacy and attribution stay with the people involved.
