01Purpose & boundaries

Clear purpose. Room to collaborate.

Less duplicated effort. More thoughtful connections. Practical ways to support one another, with each community's autonomy intact.

In scope↗

Connections that serve communities.

  • Founder and organizer exchanges; shared learning.
  • Consent-based introductions and knowledge or resource sharing.
  • Small joint initiatives, mutual support, and opportunities for young builders.
Out of scope—

Not a replacement. Not a broker.

  • Running, absorbing, or speaking on behalf of member communities.
  • Commercial brokerage, paid lead generation, or selling member access.
  • Mandatory commitments, guaranteed funding or reach, or sharing private materials without permission.

02How we collaborate

Start small. Learn together.

An early-stage, relationship-led start. Begin with trusted introductions, welcome new perspectives, and build confidence through useful work.

  1. 01 /

    Introduce your work

    Share your community, what you can offer, and what you are looking for.

  2. 02 /

    Find common ground

    Explore a relevant connection. Participation is always a choice, never an obligation.

  3. 03 /

    Agree on one initiative

    Clarify roles, time commitments, attribution, and what may be shared before starting.

  4. 04 /

    Learn and share back

    Reflect together. Share useful experience with permission, then consider the next step.

03Looking ahead

Three directions. One shared purpose.

Support the people who build communities. These are directions we are working toward, not promises of outcomes already delivered.

01 /

Connect founders

Help early-stage community founders exchange experience, receive feedback, and discover collaboration opportunities.

Experience → shared learning
02 /

Amplify with experienced partners

Invite established community partners to share knowledge and extend the reach of worthwhile, public-benefit work.

Experience → wider impact
03 /

Support young builders

Offer emerging builders meaningful roles, mentorship, and opportunities to develop work and visibility through real collaboration.

Opportunity → lasting capability
